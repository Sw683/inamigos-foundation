About InAmigos Foundation

Founded on September 23, 2020 by Govind Shukla in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh

InAmigos Foundation was started with a heartfelt mission: to unite youth, professionals, and students to solve critical socio-economic problems at the grassroots level. Guided by empathy and operational transparency, we organize active on-ground campaigns across 28 states in India.

Section 8 Registered 80G & 12A Tax Exempt NITI Aayog Darpan Listed ISO 9001:2015 Certified

Our Ongoing Initiatives

Explore the six core pillars through which we bring positive change

Child Education

Project Bachpanshala

Provides education, digital literacy, school books, and learning kits to underprivileged children living in slums and rural areas.

Impact: 15,000+ Children Enrolled
Hunger Relief

Project Seva

Fights hunger and poverty by distributing nutritious cooked meals, clean drinking water, dry rations, and winter blankets to homeless families.

Impact: 100,000+ Meals Served
Animal Welfare

Project Jeev

Cares for street animals by organizing daily feeding drives, medical rescue support, anti-rabies vaccinations, and reflective safety collar drives.

Impact: 50,000+ Street Animals Fed
Women Empowerment

Project Udaan

Raises awareness regarding menstrual hygiene, distributes sanitary pads, and supports rural Self-Help Groups (SHGs) with vocational skills.

Impact: 30,000+ Women Supported
Environment

Project Prakriti

Protects our planet through nationwide tree plantation drives, seed-bombing, cleanliness campaigns, and anti-plastic awareness.

Impact: 75,000+ Trees Planted
Youth Leadership

Project Vikas

Empowers college students and interns with hands-on experience in content writing, digital communications, graphic design, and social work.

Impact: 5,000+ Interns Trained
